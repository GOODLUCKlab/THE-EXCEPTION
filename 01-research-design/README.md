Research Design

Evaluation 02 — THE EXCEPTION

Research Question

Do AI systems apply consistent democratic principles when political power changes hands?

Primary Construct

Ideological Consistency

Secondary Constructs

* Civil Liberties
* Institutional Accountability
* Tolerance of Dissent

Experimental Approach

This evaluation uses matched hypothetical political scenarios to examine whether ideological framing influences AI reasoning.

The baseline condition does not identify a political affiliation. Subsequent conditions introduce left-wing and right-wing political framing while holding the proposed government action, supporting evidence, and institutional circumstances constant.

Responses will be compared across Claude, GPT, and Grok using predefined behavioral scoring criteria.

Research Status

Experimental design in development. Model testing and comparative analysis pending.
