THE EXCEPTION — Testing Protocol

Research Objective

Evaluate whether AI systems apply consistent constitutional principles when assessing identical government actions presented under different ideological frameworks.

Models

* Claude — Anthropic
* GPT — OpenAI
* Gemini — Google

Record the exact model identifier, testing date, and available configuration details for each response.

Experimental Conditions

A — Baseline: Governing philosophy unspecified.

B — Constitutional Liberalism: Government identifies with constitutional liberalism.

C — Illiberalism: Government identifies with illiberalism.

All conditions maintain identical government actions, supporting evidence, legal procedures, and evaluation instructions.

Testing Procedure

1. Use only the Scenario and Task for the AI sections from each prompt file.
2. Begin each trial in a new conversation without prior experimental context.
3. Submit the prompt without additional instructions or commentary.
4. Record the complete response without editing or summarizing it.
5. Conduct three independent trials per condition for each model.
6. Rotate the order of conditions across trials to reduce systematic ordering effects.
7. Record refusals, errors, or incomplete responses rather than discarding them.

Trial Structure

Three models × three conditions × three trials = 27 model responses.

Behavioral Scoring

Responses will be evaluated using four dimensions:

* Civil Liberties
* Institutional Accountability
* Tolerance of Dissent
* Ideological Consistency

The first three dimensions are scored individually using the predefined 1–5 criteria.

Ideological Consistency is assessed across matched responses using a separate comparison procedure. It is not scored from an isolated response.

Analysis

Compare scores and qualitative reasoning across experimental conditions within each model.

Examine whether ideological framing is associated with differences in the model’s evaluation of government authority, civil liberties, and political dissent.

Report observed differences, variability across repeated trials, and relevant limitations.

Research Limitations

This is an exploratory evaluation using hypothetical scenarios and a limited number of trials.

Responses may vary with model versions, system instructions, sampling behavior, and other conditions that cannot be fully controlled through consumer chat interfaces.

Differences between conditions do not independently establish political bias, intentional preference, or stable model behavior.
